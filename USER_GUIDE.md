# Resonate AI — User Guide

Welcome to Resonate AI. This guide will walk you through using the app to get your audio cleaned and ready to publish.

## What Resonate AI does

You upload audio or video files. Resonate AI converts them to MP3 and our team cleans them up — removing background noise, hum, mouth clicks, and plosives. You'll get an email with a download link for the cleaned MP3 within **24–48 hours**.

## Getting started

### 1. Create your account

1. Open **https://resonate.aaravlabs.com** in your browser.
2. Click **Send a file** in the top-right corner.
3. On the next page, enter your name, email address, and a password (at least 8 characters).
4. Click **Create account**. You're in.

### 2. Sign back in later

1. Go to **https://resonate.aaravlabs.com**.
2. Click **Sign in** in the top-right.
3. Enter your email and password.
4. Click **Sign in**.

### 3. Upload your files

1. Once you're signed in, you'll see your **Dashboard**.
2. Find the upload box near the top of the page.
3. You can either:
   - **Drag and drop** one or more files straight onto the box, **or**
   - **Click the box** to browse your computer and select files.
4. You can select multiple files at once — drop them all in together.
5. Watch the progress list below the box as each file uploads and gets converted to MP3.
6. Once a batch finishes you can click **Add more** to queue another round.

### 4. Wait for the email

That's it. As soon as your files finish uploading:

- You'll see an on-screen confirmation banner.
- Our team is automatically notified.
- Within **24–48 hours**, you'll receive an email with a secure download link to your cleaned MP3 files. The link is unique to your account and expires after 7 days.

You don't need to babysit the upload — once each batch finishes you can leave the page. We'll email you when it's ready.

## What file types are supported?

You can upload almost anything that contains audio:

**Audio**
MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, AAC, OGG, OPUS, AIFF, ALAC, WMA, AMR

**Video** (we extract the audio and convert it to MP3)
MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM, AVI, WMV, FLV, 3GP, TS

You can upload up to **25 files at once**, with each file up to **500 MB**.

## Checking your uploads

After signing in, scroll down on the Dashboard to the **Queue** section. You'll see:

- The file you uploaded
- Its size and upload date
- The MP3 version we're creating
- A status badge that says **In progress** (waiting) or **Ready** (done)
- A **Download** button on any file that's marked **Ready**

## Signing out

Click the **Sign out** button in the top-right corner of the Dashboard.

## Common questions

**How long does it take?**
Up to 24–48 hours. Most files are returned sooner.

**What does "cleaning" mean?**
We remove background noise, hiss, hum, clicks, pops, and other unwanted sounds, then balance the levels so it sounds cleaner and ready to publish.

**Why MP3?**
MP3 is a universal format that plays everywhere. We standardize everything to MP3 so you get a consistent, reliable file every time.

**Can I upload a video?**
Yes — drop in a video file and we'll extract the audio and convert it to MP3 automatically.

**What if I uploaded the wrong file?**
Just upload the correct one again. Each upload is processed independently.

**Is my file safe?**
Yes. Your files are stored privately in our secure storage and only our cleaning team can access them.
